# Recruiter Pitch Guide

A 3-4 sentence elevator pitch Vincent can use when a recruiter asks "tell me about yourself" - or that he can paste into a LinkedIn DM to a recruiter or hiring manager about a specific role.

## Format

Output as Markdown in `recruiter_pitch.md`. Two variants:

### Variant 1 - Phone screen / "tell me about yourself"

3-4 sentences spoken aloud in ~30 seconds:

1. **Who I am right now** - current role + employer in one breath.
2. **Track record** - one sentence that ties two career chapters together with a quantified outcome.
3. **Why this role** - one sentence that connects the JD's #1 priority to one of Vincent's signature wins.
4. *(Optional)* **What I'm looking for** - one sentence on what kind of role he wants next.

### Variant 2 - LinkedIn DM / cold outreach (≤500 chars)

3 sentences max, no greeting required:

1. State the role being targeted and the strongest credibility signal.
2. State the specific experience that maps to the JD's most critical requirement.
3. Ask one question or propose one next step (a 15-min call, a portfolio link, a referral).

## Tone

Recruiter pitches lean confident - but specific, not boastful. The line between "I led X" and "we delivered X" is real; pick the one that's truthful for the engagement and stick with it.

## Examples (templates, not deliverables)

### Template - Phone screen variant
> I'm currently a System Architect on Cognizant's Managed Services portfolio supporting PwC's US Assurance technology - I've been there since 2018, and before that I spent nearly two decades at Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz as their Manager of Application Architecture. Across both engagements I've led modernization programs that shipped real numbers - 40% performance gains, 35% defect reductions, and seven-figure cost-out wins. The {role} caught my attention because {hook tied to JD's priority}, which is exactly the kind of work I led when I {Vincent's matching achievement}. I'm looking for a role where {forward-looking interest pulled from preferences}.

### Template - LinkedIn DM variant
> Came across the {role} at {company}. I've been architecting AI-assisted engineering and cloud-native programs for PwC's US Assurance team since 2018, including {specific anchor that matches their JD}. Open to a 15-minute call if there's mutual interest?

## What to leave out

- Salary expectations (handle in a separate conversation)
- Why you're leaving / dissatisfied (never lead with negatives)
- Long credentials list - save for the resume
- Anything from the master profile's Flags section
